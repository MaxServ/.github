# About MaxServ

We are MaxServ, a digital agency from Waalwijk in the Netherlands. We build and manage content platforms, e-commerce platforms and open source multisite solutions for clients. We do that with a team of specialists who know their craft and enjoy sharing it. Together, we create Digital Masterpieces.

### We love open source

Open source is at the heart of everything we build. TYPO3, Magento and Solr are the foundations of our daily work, and we like to give something back. You will find our extensions, tools and fixes in this organisation, and we contribute to the projects we rely on through pull requests, bug reports and community work.

### We build content platforms

TYPO3 is our home ground. From a single website to a multisite platform with dozens of sites, we build content platforms that editors enjoy working with and that last for years.

### We do e-commerce

We build web shops on Magento and Hyvä that are fast, maintainable and ready to grow. Search is part of that: we build our own Solr-based search solutions for both content and commerce.

### We connect systems

A platform rarely stands on its own. We connect web shops, websites, back-office systems through integrations and iPaaS, and we help clients choose and set up the right online setup.

### We keep it running

Managed hosting, updates and support are part of the deal. Our operations team keeps platforms secure and up to date long after go-live.
